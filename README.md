# BingeFlix 🎬

BingeFlix is a movie, TV show, and anime discovery website.

It is being built as a collaborative project to make finding something good to watch a little easier.

## What can you do on BingeFlix?

-  Search for movies, shows, and anime
-  Explore by genre and type
-  Give your own verdict
-  Add titles to your watchlist
-  View movie and show details
-  See what other people think
-  Keep track of your ratings
-  Use it on desktop and mobile

## Rating System

Instead of the usual star ratings, BingeFlix has four simple verdicts:

- **Perfection** – Loved it
-  **Go For It** – Definitely worth watching
-  **Timepass** – Good enough for a casual watch
-  **Skip It** – Probably not worth your time
